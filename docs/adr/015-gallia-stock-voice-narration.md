# 015 — Gallia narration joins M8

The owner added narration to M8 on 2026-10-23: a deep, clear, restrained documentary voice reading the six existing beat bodies verbatim, with quiet ambience, no paid APIs and no real-person cloning. A 9.85-second stock Kokoro `bm_george` sample at speed .92 was made available at `/dev/narration`; after hearing it the owner approved proceeding with all six. This extends the audio work in ADR 009 and creates a narrow generated-narration exception to the PD/CC0-only asset rule, rather than relabelling generated output public domain. Tool/model/voice grants, output provenance and owner approval remain separate.

Generate assets offline with Apache-2.0 `kokoro-js` 1.2.1, the pinned Apache-2.0 Kokoro v1.0 ONNX q8 snapshot and the same reviewed stock voice binary. No reference recording, voice clone, hosted inference endpoint, account or paid API is used. Encode mono 24kHz MP3 using a temporary free SoundFile/libsndfile environment; generator code and weights never enter the app bundle. Each recording has a transcript SHA-256, measured duration and scoped `generated-narration` manifest approval. CI ties the transcript to the unchanged beat body; narration counts inside the unchanged <2.5 MiB total-region budget. See `docs/m8-narration-review.md` for exact source licences and limits.

`AudioDirector` owns a single disposable `NarrationTrack` alongside its existing ambience/cues. Trusted activation starts the current beat; synchronous beat subscriptions cancel the old stream before replacement, and generation guards ignore stale events even after re-entry. Narration and ambience have separate persisted switches plus the existing master mute. Beds/cues are ducked to .18 of their authored gains while narration loads/plays. Autoplay waits for the actual end plus one second, with fresh reading-time fallback when narration is unavailable, muted, disabled, fails or stalls. Startup and lost-end watchdogs prevent indefinite waiting; text remains visible and reduced-motion rendering retains its existing path. This does not complete M8's missing art, final mix/pacing or target-device acceptance.
