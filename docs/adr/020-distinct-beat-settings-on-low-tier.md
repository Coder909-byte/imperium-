# 020. Retain distinct beat settings on low tier

The owner's Italia review requested distinct images in all six beats, plus removal of overlapping/duplicated personnel. Adding three approved setting plates exposed a concrete renderer limitation: low tier selected four planes globally before beat visibility, leaving the new hall, mustering ground and departure street black. The earlier ready/overflow audit did not catch missing backgrounds; visual inspection did. This is a demonstrated content blocker, not speculative scene expansion.

Keep the existing global selection whenever it supplies scenery for every beat, preserving Gallia's accepted selection and ADR 012 composites. Otherwise cache the union of each beat's tier-selected planes, then cap the current visible set at four on low tier. When that low-tier cache spans more than four planes, transitions cut rather than crossfade across settings, so outgoing/incoming artwork cannot exceed the four rendered-plane cap. High-tier/reduced-motion rules, camera, audio, rigs and content/engine boundaries remain intact. Numerical tests and every-beat browser assertions cover missing settings, co-visible limits and unchanged actual Gallia selection. No content import or civilisation-specific engine branch is introduced.

Alternative-setting textures remain scene-owned and decoded eagerly through existing loading/cleanup; more cached images do not mean more simultaneous rendered planes, but they do increase texture memory. Italia's seven planes are about 29 MiB of decoded RGBA before other scene textures; actual phone/GPU performance remains unverified under M11. Unique referenced downloads stay under the unchanged 2.5 MiB cap by encoding a separate Italia copy of the already-approved 12-second CC0 wind loop as MP3; Gallia's WAV, existing art exports and every narration file remain untouched. Metadata/provenance records the lossy conversion and does not claim independent audible acceptance.
