## Composition and budget

Temporary layers map to four essential physical planes per view, within the existing low-tier cap. Final source cuts should share atmosphere but distinguish the **river/road** and **town/civic** visual families. Author a generous horizontal framing, grounded figures and alpha edges for desktop and the narrow crop. Camera motion, source access, muted/reading-time behavior, reduced motion and quality degradation reuse the accepted engine. Every final beat still needs actual visual review after replacement.
